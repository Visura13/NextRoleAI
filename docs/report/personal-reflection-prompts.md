# Personal reflection prompts — student writing only

Write the assessed personal reflection yourself. Do not paste AI-generated prose. Aim for approximately one page in the final report and use concrete examples from your commits, tests, and demo.

- What did you personally design and implement?
- Which technical decision was hardest, and what evidence changed your mind?
- Describe one bug or failed CI run, how you diagnosed it, and what you learned.
- How did building both React and Flutter clients change your view of shared API design?
- What did you learn about PostgreSQL constraints, authorization, controlled agents, and safe failure?
- Where did AI assistance help, where was it wrong or incomplete, and how did you verify or reject it?
- What would you improve with another week, and why?
- What evidence demonstrates that you understand and can modify the submitted work?

End with your own signed declaration in the format required by the assignment specification.
